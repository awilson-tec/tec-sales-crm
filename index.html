Connect Google Drive

One-time setup — takes about 10 minutes. After this, one click saves your backup straight to Google Drive.

Step 1 — Go to console.cloud.google.com and sign in with your Google account
Step 2 — Click Select a project → New Project → name it TEC CRM → click Create
Step 3 — In the left menu go to APIs & Services → Library → search Google Drive API → click it → click Enable
Step 4 — Go to APIs & Services → OAuth consent screen → choose External → fill in App name (TEC CRM) and your email → Save
Step 5 — Go to APIs & Services → Credentials → click + Create Credentials → OAuth Client ID
Step 6 — Choose Web application → Under Authorized JavaScript origins click + Add URI and paste: loading...
Step 7 — Click Create → copy the Client ID that appears (ends in .apps.googleusercontent.com)
Step 8 — Paste it below and click Save
